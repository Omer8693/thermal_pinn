\chapter{Results}
\label{chap:results}

This chapter presents results across ten unique benchmark geometries.
The first section covers an adaptive-$k$ study on four 2D domains with NAS
architecture transfer.
The remaining sections follow the canonical study: three 2D domains, four 3D
domains, and the Thermal Fin — a 2D steady-state benchmark from the RBniCS
library that we adapted to a 3D transient quenching scenario.
This ordering exposes how prediction accuracy changes as geometric complexity
increases.
The FEM reference solution serves as the ground truth in all cases.
All reported errors measure the deviation of the PINN prediction from that
reference.
